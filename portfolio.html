01About

Offensive mindset, defensive purpose.

I am an MSc Cyber Security student at Lancaster University (NCSC and BCS accredited), after a Bachelor's in Cybersecurity and Ethical Hacking at EFREI and two years of computer engineering at UCAC-ICAM.

My work so far has focused on web and infrastructure penetration testing: scoping, testing, scoring findings with CVSS and writing reports that a client can act on. Alongside that I investigate incidents on Windows and Linux systems, hunt bugs on YesWeHack and HackerOne, and build tooling in Python.

Outside work I compete in CTFs, am a member of LUHack, Lancaster University's hacking society, and play golf and swim competitively.
